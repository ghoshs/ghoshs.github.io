Bio

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Health NLP Lab headed by Carsten Eickhoff in the University of Tübingen. My research is supported by the AI & Data Science Fellowship Program at the university. My interest lies in building systems that can extract, organize and reason with information systems while maintaining transparency and accountability to the end user. I am particularly excited about what this means for AI in critical domains. I obtained my PhD from Max Planck Institute for Informatics and Saarland University, under the supervision of Simon Razniewski and Gerhard Weikum.
